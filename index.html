    Questions

    Before you book.

    Do you design logos yourself?

    I lead the brand thinking, tone, copy and the overall direction. For logo and detailed packaging artwork, I work with designers I trust and manage them for you, so you only deal with me.

    How much does it cost?

    Every project is a fixed price based on what you need. Use "Build your kit" above, bring the list to our call, and you'll get a written quote within 48 hours.

    We're a small business. Is this for us?

    Yes. The Brand Check-up was made for small brands that want to start small and see results before spending more.

    Can you do English + Arabic, or English + French?

    Yes, I can arrange bilingual versions with a native-language partner. Tell me on the call so it's in the plan from day one.

    How do we pay?

    Usually 50% to start and 50% on handover. International payments are fine.
